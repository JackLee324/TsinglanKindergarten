QLS PLATFORM — FINDING B5 (requestId CORRELATION): RESOLVED
generated: 2026-09-24T02:31:45Z

ORIGINAL FINDING (audit Q-5): the requestId in the response HEADER differed
from the requestId in the response BODY, making log correlation unreliable.

METHOD: send a KNOWN, caller-chosen id and see what comes back.
  $ curl -H 'x-request-id: deadbeef-1111-2222-3333-444455556666' .../api/resources/00000000-...

  header: x-request-id: deadbeef-1111-2222-3333-444455556666
  header: x-log-trace-id: f806a133-71de-48de-a1bf-fd6e1af457ae
  body:   {"error":{"code":"UNAUTHORIZED",...,
           "requestId":"deadbeef-1111-2222-3333-444455556666",...}}

RESULT: header and body carry the IDENTICAL requestId, and the CALLER'S
supplied value is preserved verbatim - the middleware honours the inbound id
rather than discarding it and generating its own.

ROOT CAUSE OF THE ORIGINAL OBSERVATION: there are TWO distinct correlation
ids in every response, and only one of them is the application's:
  x-request-id     - the application's request id; matches the body
  x-log-trace-id   - the PLATFORM's own log-trace id, a different value
They are separate identifiers with separate purposes and are NOT expected to
be equal. Their coexistence was misread as a mismatch. No defect remains.

The 401 (rather than 404) for an unauthenticated request to /api/resources/...
is correct: the global AuthGuard rejects before routing reaches the handler,
which is the intended fail-closed behaviour.
